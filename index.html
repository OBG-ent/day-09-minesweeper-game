    How to play
    • Click / tap a square to reveal it. Numbers show how many mines touch that square.
    • Right-click (or turn on Flag mode / long-press on mobile) to flag suspected mines.
    • Your first click is always safe — mines are placed after it.
    • Win by revealing every safe square, then chase your best time.
